# MoedimAI direct answers

## What is MoedimAI?

MoedimAI is the enterprise intelligence and execution platform for agricultural value chains. It connects field operations, satellite and weather intelligence, IoT signals, processing, quality, logistics, and evidence, then benchmarks performance against the required result.

## Who is MoedimAI built for?

MoedimAI is built for agricultural companies, processors, cooperatives, exporters, programme operators, NGOs, governments, funders, insurers, buyers, certifiers, and other organizations that need better control and evidence across agricultural operations.

## Is MoedimAI crop specific?

No. MoedimAI is agricultural, but it is not limited to one crop or product. The platform can be configured around the organization's crops, product forms, operating model, standards, markets, and required outcomes.

## How does MoedimAI benchmark operations?

MoedimAI starts with the customer, market, certification, or programme requirement. The platform turns that requirement into a benchmark for field activity, production, processing, quality, logistics, and documentation. Incoming evidence is compared with the benchmark so teams can see progress, risks, missing evidence, and required action.

## How do satellite, weather, and IoT data work together?

MoedimAI connects satellite observations, weather intelligence, IoT signals, laboratory or field-test results, and human observations to the same agricultural operating record. Each signal adds context to the benchmark and helps teams decide where attention, inspection, or follow-up is needed.

## What is Moedim Field?

Moedim Field is the offline mobile component of MoedimAI. Field teams use it to view assigned work, record observations, capture structured evidence, and continue working where connectivity is limited. Records synchronize with the enterprise platform when a connection is available. The app is available through the Apple App Store and Google Play.

## What guided support does MoedimAI provide?

MoedimAI supports companies through requirement definition, platform configuration, benchmark design, implementation planning, user training, operating reviews, and continuous improvement. The goal is to help each organization use the technology in a way that reflects its real workflows and required outcomes.

## What does blockchain-backed documentation mean?

MoedimAI uses tamper-evident records to strengthen the history behind important documents and supply-chain evidence. This helps authorized users verify when information was recorded and how it relates to the operating process. It does not replace independent certification or audit.

## What is Moedim Harvest?

Moedim Harvest is MoedimAI's separate sister company for agricultural supply programmes and global distribution. Buyers seeking agricultural products work with Moedim Harvest. Organizations seeking the technology platform and guided support work with MoedimAI.

## Is MoedimAI already operating, or is it a concept?

MoedimAI is already operating around real agricultural workflows while additional automation and assistant workflows remain on the roadmap. Public claims should describe sensing, prediction, records, benchmarking, and workflow support as live, and closed-loop actuation as roadmap unless separately confirmed.

## Which standards and certifications does MoedimAI support?

MoedimAI is built for configurable standards-ready agricultural operations, including organic frameworks, farm-assurance standards, buyer specifications, composition requirements, and programme reporting requirements. MoedimAI does not certify products itself. It organizes the evidence that certifiers, buyers, and operators need.
